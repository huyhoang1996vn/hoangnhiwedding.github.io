Our Story

Our story began in Da Nang, where two colleagues met at the same company, never knowing that a simple meeting would become the beginning of happy ending. 

Our first date took place in Hanoi, after a memorable flash trip that turned a friendship into something more. What followed was four beautiful years of love, laughter, and countless memories. 

Then came a new chapter: he moved to Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, for work, while she continued her journey as a cabin crew member in Ho Chi Minh City. Though miles apart, their hearts remained close, proving that true love is worth every journey and every wait. 

And now, after all the miles, all the moments apart, and all the love that kept them together, he has come back to her. 
This time, there are no more goodbyes—only a promise to walk through life hand in hand.
